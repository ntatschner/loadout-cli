# LoadStack

LoadStack is the heart of the Context page: what a session on this project would start with, from `loadout instructions explain`. One bar holds every loaded specialist as a segment sized by its tokens, against a mark for the token budget. Past the mark the bar is hatched in Needs and the words say how far over it is.

- The big number is the total; beside it, "within", "near" or "over the budget of …".
- Always-loaded specialists are hatched, and come first; the rest follow by size.
- Under the bar, the same specialists as a list with their tokens and **why each is loaded**. Hovering or focusing a segment lights its row, and the other way round. Each title links to the specialist.
- "Considered and left out" folds away the ones that were not loaded, with why.
- Segments are buttons with their name and tokens as labels, so the bar is readable without seeing it.
