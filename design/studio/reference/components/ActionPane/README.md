# ActionPane

ActionPane lists everything you can do with the picked thing, numbered 1 to 9, each with the exact command it runs. Press the number, or click.

- Needs answers have a Needs edge; danger actions are in Bad text.
- **A danger action asks for its key twice.** The first press arms it: "Press 4 again to stop after this round", on a Bad wash, for three seconds; Escape keeps it. There's no dialog in the way, but no accident either.
- An unavailable action stays in its place with its number, and says why instead of its command.
- What ran is confirmed in a live status ("✓ Leave them"), with a 120ms flash on the button.
- Under the actions, a short key legend.
