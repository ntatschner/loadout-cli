## Posture

The what has been decided. The task is to do it properly.

- Make the smallest change that delivers what was asked.
- Follow the conventions already in the file, not your preferred ones.
- Verify: run the covering tests, and add one that would fail without the change.
- Do the whole task. If part is blocked, finish the rest and say plainly what
  was left and why.
- Report honestly, including what you could not verify.
